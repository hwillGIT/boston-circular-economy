## Source

PROPOSED. The UX Strategy doc plans 1 city-staff persona but names none. The team docs say the City wants low effort, low maintenance and its own design standards. The City reviewer is a placeholder for the Circular Economy administrator role. It is not a real person and is not confirmed with the team.

## Why this rank

The City owns the brand and will take over the app. A reviewer must trust what is shown, with no new daily work.

## Goals

- See that every claim has a source
- Remove a wrong or unsafe listing fast
- Know the cost to keep it running

## Main journey

1. **Start.** Gets a catalog change for review.
2. **Read.** Sees source and check date per row.
3. **Decide.** Approves, asks for a fix or rejects.
4. **Release.** Maintainer ships the build.
5. **Audit.** Reads the change log.

## Edge cases

- Reviewer is not technical: use a plain list, not code.
- Reviewer is away: a named backup approves.
- City rule changes (for example recycling): link and re-check.

## Success measure

A reviewer can approve or reject one change in under 10 minutes with no help.
